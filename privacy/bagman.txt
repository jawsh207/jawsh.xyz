Privacy Policy for Bagman: Crust & Consequence
Effective date: October 13, 2026
This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Bagman: Crust & Consequence Android app (com.jawsh.bagman), made by [Developer name] ("we", "us").
Information we collect
We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat, and we don't run any servers that receive information about you.
Game progress
Your progress and settings (checkpoint, items, upgrades, sound options) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
Advertising
The app shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob, after a boss battle and after your character is defeated. To show and measure ads, limit repeats and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, including:
your device's advertising ID
your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
device information, such as model, operating system and language
ad interactions, such as views and taps
diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information from the ads software
This information is collected and handled by Google under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More on how Google uses data from apps that show its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
Your choices:
Where required by law (such as in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized.
You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
